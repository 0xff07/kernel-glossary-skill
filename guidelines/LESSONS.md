# LESSONS

The incident history behind the rules, dated. Nothing here is required reading for a writer; a rule states its requirement and this file keeps the story. A finding lands here first, and it changes a rule only when the same class recurs after the lesson was already in a writer's brief. Settled boundaries go to `guidelines/rules/WAIVERS.md` through the user; refuted campaign claims go to the campaign spec as dated amendments.

## 2026-06 and 07: the first corpora and the samples

- The June corpora (acpi, pci, sound, usb4, dp) were written under a single 1,124-line SKILL.md that said, among other things, "show the relevant code, then explain it", "explain each block in the prose between them", and "interleaved with the prose that walks through it". Those sentences did not survive the July split of the rules and were restored in `WRITING.md` on 2026-09-02.
- A draft of the mm VMA overview read plausibly and was wrong at the tree: it claimed `vm_mm` is written once (the fork path writes it again), misstated the anonymous `vm_pgoff` encoding, and miscounted `vma_set_range()` call sites. The rebuilt page verified every claim symbol by symbol; a later audit still found a false "invoked from exactly one place" (four callers) and an off-by-one count. Sample pages calibrate form; correctness is established only by re-running FACT-03 against the tree, on every page.
- Four frozen mm pages became the writer's exemplar in July. Measured on 2026-09-02 they were the least prose-dense corpus in the repository (52 prose words per code block) and the most numeral-dense (10 numerals per thousand words), and named no member beside 7 of their 19 definition excerpts. They were retired in favour of `docs/sound/` and deleted with the restructure of 2026-09-02.
- The fresh-eyes prose sweep by a second agent was retired: its premise (a writer misses its own blind spots on re-reading) is true but irrelevant, because the sweeps are procedure, not perception. Measured, a writer using the prose view fixed 32 of 35 defects in its own draft, including 20 label-colons it had re-read twice without seeing, and a fresh-context sweep afterwards found 2 items on a 2,000-line page at a cost of 290,000 to 460,000 tokens. The writer sweeps its own prose; the orchestrator re-runs the mechanics.

## 2026-07 and 08: the checks that could not fire

- Four classes shipped for pages at a time behind a pattern that structurally could not see them: the mid-paragraph label-colon behind a line-anchored grep (the anchored form caught 12% of known hits, the unanchored form 100%); figure annotations behind the fence-stripping prose view; headings behind a prose view that dropped every `#` line; and the whole catalog region behind an exemption that generalized one carve-out into a blind spot. The discipline that came out of it is in ROUTINE-01: for every rule that binds a region, name the mechanism that reaches it.
- A permissive excerpt verifier that resynchronized on any mismatch reported 44 of 44 units correct over two fabricated comment terminators. Verifiers resynchronize only after a declared elision, and every helper script is negative-controlled before its clean result is believed.
- A writer's excerpt verifier was silently overwritten by a sibling writer's script in the shared scratchpad and reported 75 blocks against a true 71; a one-line grep exposed it. Scratch files are namespaced per page.
- A writer's LINKS table came back carrying another page's spans for the same reason. The extractor emits the table; the writer judges its rows.
- A PLOT-04 derivation compressed a 2,645-line page to 1,268 lines, kept its iterator-helper symbols in the catalog while dropping their DETAILS sections, and landed at 0.73 fenced blocks per catalog entry. The parity audit catches the desync mechanically; the disposition list is what makes a removal legitimate.
- FACT-03's history: a helper "invoked from exactly one place" had four callers; a lead claimed each wrapper "forwards to exactly one" primitive while the page's own table showed one calling two; a polish pass strengthened "the accessors mediate every flag change" into "take the write lock before every flag change" above an excerpt whose comment reads "needs no locking". Universal quantifiers are enumerations, per-member claims are mappings, headings are claims.
- 2026-08-20, the sound campaign restart: the user compared a rewritten page against its predecessor and found it worse: "the original pages provide more insights and observations. The one you wrote is pretty bad. You just keep counting numbers." The orchestrator's own diagnosis: counts are what reliably survive verification, so writers produce what gets checked, and nothing in the loop asked what a reader understands. The rule it recorded, "counts serve claims; a count that serves no claim is noise; every DETAILS section states what the reader should understand", stayed in that campaign's spec on an unmerged branch and never reached the skill until `WRITING.md`.

## 2026-08-29 to 09-02: the rule split and what followed

- The rules were split into 41 files with INPUT, OUTPUT and PASS CRITERIA, the adjudications into five waivers files, and the humanizer rewriter switched on with a conflict rule that "the opening sentence must say something the heading does not". BAN-08 (run-on enumerations to tables) landed the same day. Measured afterwards with the member generator: definition excerpts with no member named beside them ran 13% to 25% in every corpus written before that window and 51% in the two batches written right after it, inside the same campaigns and with the same model. The causes, each read literally: BAN-08's "what the members share" applied to excerpts instead of tables, BAN-02's text read as banning the colon that introduces an excerpt (the June corpus introduced 98 of every 100 excerpts that way; every later corpus none), and the rewriter rule barred the opener from the heading's purpose.
- 2026-09-01: a page's excerpts were byte-exact and too short (a signature plus an opening brace); the excerpt-sufficiency criterion in measured-criteria.md records the medians.
- 2026-09-01: the dense-reference-table adjudication was settled against exemption (WAIVERS.md).
- 2026-09-02: the xhci port-arrays page's paragraphs beside `struct xhci_port` said "the first five fields are what the construction pass writes" while a nine-row field table 360 lines earlier held the purposes. That became PAGE-07, and with the lead-and-opener finding on the next page ("carries three groups of writable control bits ...", "one field set with three writers", "six write behaviours in one 32-bit word") PAGE-08; both were folded into `WRITING.md` in the restructure of the same day.
- 2026-09-02: a writer left its emitted LINKS table in scratch with every judgement cell empty and wrote a summary into the dossier, the fourth recorded instance; the next writer persisted its table, but four rows whose quoted source line contained a bare `|` had their kind pushed into the wrong column, because the extractor escapes pipes in the span cell and not in the disk-line cell. A brief told a writer that `usb_port_suspend` issues the SuperSpeed wake-mask request; the tree places it in `hub_suspend`. A page said a dispatch "decodes the three payloads it understands" where the code decodes four. A figure generator left an empty line inside two-line figure subtitles.
- 2026-09-02, the restructure: the writer's mandatory reading had grown from 11,600 words in June to about 43,000 (42 rule files, 341 rule items, 170 pass criteria), almost all of it prohibitions and checking procedure, and the text that survived every check was the countable residue. `WRITING.md` (what a page is for, read first) and `BANS.md` (every pattern, fix and exemption as one table) replaced fourteen files; the harness moved behind the exit suite; the humanizer went off; the samples were deleted; the passes kept their procedure and lost their narrative, which is this file.
- 2026-09-03: two xhci pages shipped 44 sentences of the shape "X rather than Y" with the negative-construction sweep at zero; the row's prose described that shape and its pattern `(,|\band)\s+(not|never)\s` could not generate it. The writer, the check and the user each read the two sentences the user named as real hits (a Y never on the table). The pattern now carries `rather than` and `instead of`, and the exempt column names the comparison whose denied alternative is a real design the reader could otherwise assume, which the read step decides. 24 unread candidates remain on usb3-device-pm, 18 on usb2-device-pm, and the earlier pages of every campaign carry the same unmeasured debt.
